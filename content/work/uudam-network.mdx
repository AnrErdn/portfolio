## THE PROBLEM

Uudam Network needed to launch with presence. A new Mongolian media outlet entering a crowded space, with no existing brand equity and a tight timeline. The risk: looking generic before anyone had a chance to know who they were.

## THE SOLUTION

A brand identity and landing page built around clarity and momentum. Clean typographic system, strong color palette, and a layout that communicated what the network covers and why it matters — without relying on photography they didn't yet have.

## Process

**01 — Brand definition.** Before touching Figma, I pushed the client on one question: what's Uudam for that existing outlets aren't? The answer — depth over speed, analysis over breaking news — shaped everything. That positioning drove the visual tone: measured, confident, not flashy.

**02 — Identity system.** I tested wordmark vs. symbol-plus-wordmark. Wordmark won — the name itself is the brand asset at this stage. Set in a customised version of Inter with adjusted letterforms to give it distinctiveness without sacrificing legibility.

**03 — Landing page.** The page needed to do one job: convert a visitor into a subscriber or follower. Hierarchy was the entire brief. Above the fold: what it is, why it matters, single CTA. Below the fold: proof — editorial sample and coverage areas.

## Outcome

The site was built and delivered. The client has not officially launched it — it's pending internal approval. The design is live as a concept at uudam-network.vercel.app.

## What I'd do differently

I'd negotiate earlier access to real editorial content. Designing with placeholder text made the hierarchy decisions feel abstract — real headlines would have forced better type scaling decisions from the start.
